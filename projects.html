Zero Cloud Home Lab

A self-hosted Kubernetes cluster built from three old Chromebooks.

🌐 Live at ayush1.xyz🔨 k3s, Docker, Cloudflare Tunnel

A 3-node k3s Kubernetes cluster built from three Chromebooks. It self-hosts Nginx, Grafana, Prometheus, Gitea, Drone CI, and Pi-hole, all exposed to the internet through Cloudflare Tunnel with zero open inbound ports and zero cloud bills.

🔨 k3s, Docker, Cloudflare Tunnel, Grafana, Prometheus, Gitea, Drone CI, Pi-hole, NFS

DeepGaze: Gesture-Controlled Dino Game

Jump in Chrome’s Dino game with a wave of your hand.

🔨 Python, OpenCV, MediaPipe

A computer-vision game controller that lets you play Chrome’s Dino game with hand gestures. OpenCV and MediaPipe track your hand in real time, and PyAutoGUI fires the jump. It was a fun way to explore gesture-based interfaces and how far lightweight CV models can go in real time.

🔨 Python, OpenCV, MediaPipe, PyAutoGUI
